## SDK / host gaps and owner decisions

1. Order-preserving delivery of event payload objects and session reads (R1, R3) and raw echo of unchanged boundary drafts (R2): SDK-level; the port cannot fix them.
2. Console output of a Go extension in headless modes (R4): PiG keeps it in a hidden log; decide on forwarding or a named exclusion.
3. Reminder lookup round trips (R6 second half) and cold negative lookup design (R7): latency vs peak memory.
